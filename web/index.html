What counts for this field

Untick a source or a gauge and it stops counting for this field, in the past as well as going forward. Nothing is deleted — tick it back on and the history returns. A gauge 30 miles away describes your home quarter far better than it describes the far end of the county.

Fields

Edits are written straight to config.json. Acres and farm save as soon as you leave the box; moving a field remaps its nearby gauges. After adding a field, run npm run backfill to pull its history.
